**Wave 1 — 14 Sept 2026 (morning).** Akshat took over all remaining work from Anushka (Stage 2)
and Harshita (frontend + integration), on top of his own, and their planning documents were moved
here.

**Wave 2 — 14 Sept 2026 (reorganisation).** Most of wave 1 was **moved back out**. Archiving a
person's brief also archived the only full account of what that person built, which is exactly
what each of them needs to prepare for judging. Those documents now live in `docs/team/` with a
banner saying they are historical and that live work has transferred to
[`docs/team/akshat-remaining.md`](../team/akshat-remaining.md).

`docs/receipts.md`, by the deck numbers, and by `pipeline/drift/publish_all.py` — so they moved to
`docs/evaluation/` alongside the other evidence.

## Stale references inside these files

The documents in this directory cite paths as they were when written, and **they have been left
that way on purpose.** Rewriting the text of a dated report falsifies the record of what was
known when. The same applies to the per-person work logs in `docs/updates/`, which are
append-only.

To resolve an old path, use the table above and the fuller redirect table in
[`docs/README.md`](../README.md).
